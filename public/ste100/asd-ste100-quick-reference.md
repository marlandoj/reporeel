# ASD-STE100 Quick Reference

This is a practical subset of the 53 writing rules in ASD-STE100 Issue 9, paraphrased for software documentation. The check is mechanical: it proves what a program can prove, not what a judge would decide. Full compliance needs the official dictionary, a free download from asd-ste100.org. ASD-STE100 is a registered trademark of ASD. RepoReel is an unofficial aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ASD.

## Section 1 — Words

| Rule | Instruction | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | Use only approved words, technical nouns, or technical verbs. | mechanical |
| 1.2 | Use an approved word only as its listed part of speech. | judgement |
| 1.3 | Use an approved word only with its approved meaning. | judgement |
| 1.4 | Use only the approved forms of verbs and adjectives. | mechanical |
| 1.5 | You can use domain words as technical nouns, for example webhook or commit. | judgement |
| 1.6 | Use an unapproved word only when it is a technical noun or part of one. | judgement |
| 1.7 | Do not use technical nouns as verbs. | judgement |
| 1.8 | Use the technical nouns of your project or industry. | judgement |
| 1.9 | When you pick a technical noun, pick a short and clear one. | judgement |
| 1.10 | No regional, slang, or jargon words as technical nouns. | judgement |
| 1.11 | One item, one name. Do not call it config here and settings there. | mechanical |
| 1.12 | You can use domain verbs as technical verbs, for example deploy or merge. | judgement |
| 1.13 | Do not use technical verbs as nouns. | judgement |
| 1.14 | Use American English spelling. | mechanical |

## Section 2 — Multi-word nouns

| Rule | Instruction | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.1 | Write multi-word nouns of three words or fewer. | mechanical |
| 2.2 | Write a long technical noun in full once, then give a short form. | judgement |

## Section 3 — Verbs

| Rule | Instruction | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.1 | Use only the verb forms that the dictionary gives. | judgement |
| 3.2 | Use only the infinitive, imperative, simple present, simple past, simple future, and participle as adjective. | judgement |
| 3.3 | Use the past participle only as an adjective, for example the cached response. | judgement |
| 3.4 | No auxiliary verbs for complex constructions. No present perfect, no is to be installed. | mechanical |
| 3.5 | Use an -ing form only as a technical noun or inside one, never as a verb. | mechanical |
| 3.6 | Active voice. Passive is legal only when the agent is unknown. | mechanical |
| 3.7 | Describe an action with a verb, not a noun. | mechanical |

## Section 4 — Sentences

| Rule | Instruction | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.1 | Write short and clear sentences. | judgement |
| 4.2 | Do not omit words or use contractions to shorten sentences. Keep articles, keep that. | mechanical |
| 4.3 | Use a vertical list for complex text. | mechanical |
| 4.4 | Use connecting words between sentences on related topics. | mechanical |
| 4.5 | Put an article or a demonstrative adjective before the noun where applicable. | mechanical |

## Section 5 — Procedural writing

| Rule | Instruction | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5.1 | Maximum 20 words per sentence. Warnings and cautions included. | mechanical |
| 5.2 | One instruction per sentence, unless two actions happen at the same time. | mechanical |
| 5.3 | Write instructions in the imperative. | mechanical |
| 5.4 | Put a required condition before the command, divided by a comma. | mechanical |
| 5.5 | Notes give information, never instructions. Notes get the 25-word limit. | mechanical |

## Section 6 — Descriptive writing

| Rule | Instruction | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6.1 | Give information gradually, one subject per sentence. | judgement |
| 6.2 | Use key words and phrases to give the text a logical structure. | judgement |
| 6.3 | Maximum 25 words per sentence. | mechanical |
| 6.4 | Group related information in paragraphs. | judgement |
| 6.5 | One topic per paragraph. | judgement |
| 6.6 | Maximum six sentences per paragraph. | mechanical |

## Section 7 — Safety instructions

| Rule | Instruction | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7.1 | Use a word that shows the risk level, for example WARNING or CAUTION. | judgement |
| 7.2 | Start with a clear command or condition. | judgement |
| 7.3 | Then give the risk or the possible result. | judgement |

## Section 8 — Punctuation and word count

| Rule | Instruction | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8.1 | All standard punctuation is legal except the semicolon. Write two sentences instead. | mechanical |
| 8.2 | Use hyphens to connect words that act as one unit. | judgement |
| 8.3 | Parentheses are legal for references, item numbers, abbreviations, plurals, explanations, and alternatives. | judgement |
| 8.4 | In a vertical list, the lead-in colon ends a sentence for word count. | judgement |
| 8.5 | Text inside parentheses counts as one word. | judgement |
| 8.6 | Count as one word each: numbers, numbers with units, abbreviations, identifiers, quoted text, titles, labels, and proper nouns. | mechanical |
| 8.7 | A hyphenated word counts as one word. | judgement |

## Section 9 — Writing practices

| Rule | Instruction | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9.1 | When a word-for-word replacement does not work, restructure the sentence. | mechanical |
| 9.2 | Use each approved word with its approved meaning and part of speech. | judgement |
| 9.3 | Do not build phrasal verbs. Write decrease, not go down. | mechanical |
| 9.4 | Keep one consistent style and terminology through the whole document. | mechanical |
| GR-1 | Keep the conjunction that. | judgement |
| GR-2 | Be careful with with. | judgement |
| GR-3 | Give pronouns a clear referent. | judgement |
| GR-4 | Prefer this plus a noun over a bare this. | judgement |
| GR-5 | Avoid false friends between languages. | judgement |
| GR-6 | Avoid Latin abbreviations. Write for example, that is, and name the items instead of etc. | mechanical |
| GR-7 | Use inclusive language. | mechanical |
| GR-8 | Use the possessive apostrophe form only when you are sure it is correct. | judgement |
